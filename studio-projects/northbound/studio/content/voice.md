# Voice: Northbound

**In one line:** a surveyor who likes you. Exact about the numbers, plain about what they mean, never loud.

The concept is "Every roast is a climb." The voice borrows the surveyor's and trail guide's words, literally and in small doses, and always attaches them to a real number.

## Traits
| Trait | Means | This | Not that |
|---|---|---|---|
| Precise | Gives the number, then says what it means for the cup. Every claim of freshness carries a date; every claim of quality carries a process or a figure. | "First crack at 8:50, 196 °C. Development ran 1:50, so it tastes bright." | "Carefully roasted to bring out the best in every bean." |
| | | "Roasted Tue 27 Oct. At your door by Friday." | "Freshly roasted and delivered fast." |
| Warm | Talks to a person with a kettle and a busy week. Explains instead of showing off, and makes leaving or pausing easy. | "If a cup tastes sharp, grind a touch finer." | "Our proprietary profiling ensures optimal extraction." |
| | | "Away next week? Skip it. Your place on the route keeps." | "Cancellations are subject to our policy." |
| Outdoorsy | Borrows the vocabulary of surveyors and trail guides, plainly. Understated, practical, never summit-conquering. | "Drop at 10:40, 207 °C. That's the summit." | "Conquer your mornings and fuel your next adventure." |
| | | "Plot your route. Skip any stop." | "Embark on your coffee journey." |

## Concept vocabulary (keep these consistent across the page)
| Thing | Say | Don't say |
|---|---|---|
| A roast, as an event | the climb, the roast | the process, the craft |
| Its temperature record | the roast curve (the map, when talking about the hero terrain) | the profile data, analytics |
| The paper that ships in the bag | the curve card | the insert, the leaflet |
| A coffee's summary on the page | a route card (Designer's term; not shown to users) | product card |
| The delivery plan | your route | your subscription settings |
| One delivery | a stop | an order, a shipment |
| Cancelling a single delivery | skip | cancel |
| Holding everything | pause (skipping every stop) | suspend |

## Use
- **Roasting:** roast curve, charge, turning point, dry end, first crack, drop, drum, cooling tray, bean temperature, drying, Maillard, development, DTR (development time ratio), roast date, roast level, batch 214.
- **Origin:** washed, honey, washing station, factory (Kenya), lot, elevation shown as "Grown at 2,000 m", latitude (5.9°N).
- **Brewing:** dose, ratio (1:16), 18 g to 288 g, grind a touch finer, V60, Chemex, espresso 18 g in and 40 g out, 93 °C, peak window.
- **Survey and trail:** climb, summit, contour, section A–A′, spot height, route, stop, waypoint, trailhead. Use them literally and sparingly: at most one or two per section.
- **Time words that do the work:** Tuesday (roast), Wednesday (ship), Friday (door), Sunday 23:59 ET (last call), 24 hours, 3 days.

## Avoid
- **Studio banned list:** revolutionary, seamless(ly), unlock, elevate, leverage, cutting-edge, game-changer, next-level, world-class, best-in-class, innovative, empower, synergy, robust, journey (as a metaphor), "in today's fast-paced world", "look no further", "we're passionate about", "solutions" used alone, and any exclamation mark in a headline.
- **Category clichés (the hipster list):** artisan, craft or crafted, hand-crafted, curated, ethically sourced, farm-to-cup, bean-to-cup, premium, perfect cup, "fuel your day", wake up and smell, morning ritual, cozy, sip, java, cup of joe, small but mighty, obsessed, "fresh-roasted" as a bare claim (give the date instead).
- **Outdoor-brand clichés:** adventure, conquer, explore, wanderlust, rugged, peak performance, "summit your morning". The climb metaphor is about measurement, not heroics.
- **Marketing tics:** "Learn more", "Submit", "Click here", "Sign up now", stacked superlatives, rhetorical question openers, "not just X, but Y" constructions.
- **Anywhere on the page:** exclamation marks (not only in headlines) and em dashes. Use a period, a comma or a colon.

## Grammar and rhythm
- **Person:** "we" for Northbound (we roast, we ship, we print), "you" for the reader. Never "users" or "our customers".
- **Sentences:** short, 6 to 14 words. One longer sentence per section is allowed when it carries a how and a why. Fragments are fine in labels and eyebrows ("Roasted Tue 27 Oct"). Contractions are normal (it's, we've, couldn't).
- **Headings:** sentence case. Display lines may carry one italic word, marked `*word*` in `copy.md` (the single warm touch, as in "a *climb*"). Full-sentence headings end with a period. No exclamation marks.
- **Numbers:** numerals for all data, days and prices ("3 days", "$21", "10:40"). Times in drum as m:ss ("8:50"). Clock times on a 24-hour clock with a zone ("Sunday 23:59 ET"). Dates as weekday, day, month ("Tue 27 Oct"); add the year only where it matters. Temperatures "207 °C" with a non-breaking space, and "207°" on map labels. Mass "340 g", elevation "2,000 m", ratio "1:16", coordinates "46.5°N". Use tabular figures wherever numbers line up.
- **Spelling:** American (color, gray, favorite). The roastery is in Michigan.
- **CTAs:** verb plus outcome. The primary action is always exactly "Start a subscription". The secondary action is "See current roasts". Link arrows are added by the Builder as aria-hidden glyphs, not typed into the copy.
- **Errors and empty states:** say what to do next, never blame the reader. Success messages say what happens next and when.
- **Honesty rule:** if the page can't show the number, it doesn't make the claim. Demo content is labeled as demo in the footer and beside the form.
